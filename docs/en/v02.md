# v0.2 records and scoped assessment

Package `0.2.0rc2` reads `schema_version: "0.2"` alongside the frozen
[v0.1 schema](schema.md). The executable definition is
[`schema_v02.py`](../../src/mceg/schema_v02.py). The
[H2 contract](../../examples/v02/h2_solubility.yaml) is a complete example;
its builder uses the previously reviewed source intervals and does not add
experimental evidence.

## What is separated

| Record | Meaning | H2 / curated example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim | Proposition, kind, scope and explicit revision; no status or evaluative evidence links | A specified formula or numerical comparison criterion |
| Evidence | Scoped source or execution record with its original result and provenance | An inspected abstract or an archived numerical pass |
| Subject | Implementation identity and optional selected-file SHA-256 manifest | Curated code, coefficients, abundance and adapter files |
| Context | Supplied quantities and settings for a calculation or execution | Temperature, pressure, composition, JAX version, tolerance |
| Condition | A source-scoped closed interval or enumerated joint samples, with revision and evidence IDs | Separate metadata/Seo temperatures; 70 curated samples |
| Assessment | Judgment bound to one Subject, Context, method revision and evidence list, with reasons | Membership in a reported range or support for one Claim revision |
| Follow-up | Human-authored action, required material and completion criteria for a specific record | Obtain the original paper and inspect the concentration convention |
| Follow-up resolution | Authored completion with Evidence references and a note; the original task remains intact | Published fit inspected; independent reconstruction remains a separate task |

Changing a proposition requires a new Claim revision. Changing code or
coefficients requires a distinct Subject. Changing the environment or requested
conditions requires a distinct Context. Earlier numerical Evidence remains
`passed` when reuse for a new target becomes `unknown`.

Records use local references. Claim and Condition identities are `(id, revision)`
pairs; all other IDs are unique within their record collection. An Assessment
cannot target an absent revision, Subject, Context, or Evidence. Source tracing
retains v0.1's cycle and completed-comparison checks. A supported Assessment
requires the corresponding evidence category, as in v0.1; that structural rule
does not establish the source's relevance or the truth of the judgment.

## Minimal data shape

The contract retains `model`, `assumptions`, `domains`, `evidence`, and
`limitations`. `domains` retains its descriptive v0.1 meaning. `subjects` and
`contexts` are nonempty lists; `conditions`, `assessments`, `follow_ups`, and
`follow_up_resolutions` default to empty lists. All fields reject unknown keys
and wrong types. The resolution field requires package rc2 or later; rc1 rejects
it even when empty. See the [compatibility policy](versioning.md).

- Claims have `id`, `revision`, `statement`, `kind`, and `scope`.
- Subjects have `id`, `implementation` (repository, revision, entry points), and
  optional `selected_files: {path: sha256}`. A null digest records an observed
  missing file, not a matching file. `model.implementation` remains the model
  declaration; the Assessment's Subject identifies the evaluated implementation.
- Contexts have `id`, `scope`, `quantities`, and `settings`. Settings are flat
  strings, booleans or finite numbers. No environment values are inferred.
- Evidence may additionally identify its `subject`, `context`, and
  `artifacts: [{source, sha256}]`. These identify recorded artifacts; a generic
  structural check does not fetch them or verify external file contents.
  Optional `check --assessment ID --root DIR` verifies local Subject and Evidence
  hashes within an explicit root; see [file verification](checks.md#verify-local-file-hashes).
- Every Assessment has `id`, `kind`, `subject`, `context`, `method`, `evidence`,
  and nonempty `reasons`. A method has `id`, `revision`, and `description`.

Assessment kinds are deliberately distinct:

| `kind` | Target | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `evidence_support` | `claim`, `claim_revision` | `status: supported` or `unknown`; `origin: imported_v0.1_declaration` or `reviewed` |
| `range_membership` | `condition`, `condition_revision` | `classification: within_reported_range`, `outside_reported_range`, or `unknown` |
| `sample_membership` | `condition`, `condition_revision` | `classification: at_recorded_sample`, `not_sampled`, or `unknown` |

`reviewed` records a supplied review; it is not a reviewer authentication or a
general scientific inference rule. IDs and hashes identify data, not independent
attestations. Preserve earlier records in Git or saved artifacts when authoring
revisions; the schema cannot prevent someone from editing a document.

## Conditions and automatic comparison

A range Condition has `id`, `revision`, `kind: range`, `quantity`, `interval`,
`coverage`, `scope`, and nonempty `evidence`. An interval has `lower`, `upper`,
`unit`, and `closure: closed`. Coverage is `marginal` or `numerical_evaluation`.
Separate marginal intervals never establish a jointly validated T/P rectangle.

A sample Condition has `kind: samples` and `samples: [Context, ...]` instead of
the range fields. Every sample explicitly describes one joint point, with the
same coordinate keys across samples. IDs and coordinates cannot duplicate.
Matching does not infer interpolation or coverage of extra Context fields.

The quantities are `temperature` (K) and the following pressure quantities
(Pa, bar, MPa or GPa):

- `pressure`, `melt_total_pressure`
- `hydrogen_fugacity`, `water_fugacity`, `helium_fugacity`,
  `carbon_monoxide_fugacity`, `methane_fugacity`
- `water_partial_pressure`, `carbon_dioxide_partial_pressure`, `nitrogen_partial_pressure`

Pressure units are converted before comparison. Quantity names remain distinct:
total melt pressure cannot supply missing hydrogen fugacity or water partial
pressure, and one species cannot supply another's input. These names record
the native driving quantities of volatile-solubility laws and their liquid
continuations. In particular, `water_partial_pressure` and `water_fugacity`
remain distinct even when supplied values happen to be equal. Log-pressure inputs
must first be converted to linear quantities by the caller; no fugacity model
or gas composition is inferred. Temperatures must be positive; pressures/fugacity
nonnegative; all values finite. Missing coordinates yield `unknown`; invalid
values or incompatible units are errors. No chemical or reference-state
conversion is implemented.

For a new H2 calculation, save a Context with a new ID:

```json
{
  "id": "h2-1700K",
  "scope": "Temperature and total melt pressure only; composition and fugacity unspecified.",
  "quantities": {
    "temperature": {"value": 1700, "unit": "K"},
    "melt_total_pressure": {"value": 0.75, "unit": "GPa"}
  }
}
```

From the MCEG checkout, with that JSON saved as `/tmp/h2-1700.json`:

```bash
mceg assess examples/v02/h2_solubility.yaml \
  --context /tmp/h2-1700.json --subject imported --output /tmp/h2-1700.yaml
mceg check /tmp/h2-1700.yaml
mceg explain /tmp/h2-1700.yaml
```

`assess` appends membership Assessments for the supplied Subject/Context to a
copy. It preserves earlier Claims, Evidence and Assessments. Repeating an
identical assessment is idempotent; reusing a Context ID with different content
or overwriting an output file is rejected. `.json` outputs are JSON; other
outputs are YAML. Membership never changes scientific support.

`check` recomputes recorded membership classifications against their own
Conditions and Contexts. A mismatch fails the check. Without a selector, the
scope is labeled `All records, including history`: the H2 example's 1750 K
result outside Seo's interval remains visible. This is not a physical-invalidity
finding. The generic `evidence_reuse` check remains UNKNOWN because no single
support Assessment is selected for an implicit current context.

Select a specific judgment with the same ID used by `explain`:

```bash
mceg check /tmp/h2-1700.yaml --assessment imported:physical_accuracy
mceg explain /tmp/h2-1700.yaml --assessment imported:physical_accuracy
```

Both commands audit only the selected Assessment's Evidence and their transitive
provenance dependencies, counting each record once. The selected Subject and
Context are shown; neither is inferred from a date or an installed environment.
Other Assessments, even those with the same Context, are not selected. Whole
contract structural validation still runs, so selection cannot hide malformed
historical references. The next-step command retains the Assessment ID.

For a support Assessment, `recorded_support` displays its stored judgment and
origin. A PASS means that the selected record says `supported`, not that the
source audit has independently re-established support. Empty Evidence leaves
the source audit UNKNOWN; a complete source trace cannot promote unknown
physical support. A selected membership Assessment is recomputed and compared
with its stored result. No generic reuse judgment is added to that selection.

## Reviewed curated adapter

The existing [curated policy](curated_assessment.md) can export these same
record types, using its original flat context format:

```bash
mceg assess --curated /path/to/exogibbs \
  --context examples/curated_gas/context.json --output /tmp/curated-v02.yaml
mceg explain /tmp/curated-v02.yaml
```

The adapter identifies observed and archived selected inputs as separate
Subjects, retains the receipt and output hashes on the existing Evidence,
enumerates the reviewed 70 joint samples, and adds a support Assessment for the
original `equilibrium_verification` Claim revision. Its method identifies both
the evaluator and review hashes. The reviewed default context is supported;
1750 K, different JAX, missing context or changed selected files leave reuse
unknown. Original declarations and execution outcomes are preserved.

This adapter executes no solver, updates no provider contract, and selects no
later lifecycle archive automatically. New numerical runs still require their
own receipt, evidence and scope review. A changed pinned contract or receipt
requires a review before the adapter will assess it.

## Migration and compatibility

```bash
mceg migrate contracts/h2_solubility.yaml --output /tmp/h2-migrated.yaml
mceg /tmp/h2-migrated.yaml
mceg explain /tmp/h2-migrated.yaml --assessment imported:physical_accuracy
```

Migration retains Claim text, including any old wording that embeds an outcome.
Each old status and evidence list becomes an `imported_v0.1_declaration`
Assessment. The Claim revision identifies the source contract hash. Its Subject
retains the declared implementation; its Context retains the prose scope with
no invented quantities. Migration alone adds no Condition or new review.

The migration record includes the original byte hash, a canonical validated-data
hash and the original Evidence IDs. `restore_v01()` reconstructs the original
validated data and checks that hash, even after adding new Claim revisions,
Evidence, conditions or Assessments. Original YAML comments/formatting are not
recreated; the source file itself is never modified. Changes to retained
historical scientific content are rejected during restoration.

`load_contract()` dispatches on the exact quoted version. `Contract` remains the
v0.1 model and rejects v0.2; `ContractV02` is explicit. Unsupported versions are
rejected. Original validation/trace commands and v0.1 status meanings remain
unchanged. The public additions are `migrate_contract`, `restore_v01`, and
`assess_conditions`; record classes are in `mceg.schema_v02`, and the reviewed
adapter is `mceg.curated.assess_curated`.

## Actionable review gaps

```bash
mceg explain examples/v02/h2_solubility.yaml --evidence experimental_fit
mceg explain examples/v02/h2_solubility.yaml --claim physical_accuracy
```

Follow-ups have `id`, `target_kind`, `target`, optional `revision`, `action`,
`requires`, `completion`, and optional `sources`. Claim/Condition targets require
a revision. Evidence/Assessment targets omit it. The H2 example records the
original full-text access gap, the fit and run-table inspection tasks, and the
physical comparison criteria still needing definition.

`explain` labels authored instructions separately from suggestions derived from
audit gaps. Access notes describe the recorded audit; they are not a fresh
availability check. Receiving a paper, adding its path, or completing a numerical
run does not silently mark a source inspected or a Claim supported. Record the
actual scoped inspection/comparison and a new Assessment after review. External
source downloading, cross-contract support propagation, arbitrary domain
inference, source ranking and general uncertainty reasoning remain outside v0.2.

### Record completed work

Providers record completion in their own contracts after reviewing the task's
criteria. This synthetic example is independent of any model or publication:

```yaml
follow_up_resolutions:
  - follow_up: inspect-source
    evidence: [reviewed-source]
    note: >-
      Source conventions checked for the recorded scope.
      Independent validation remains a separate task.
```

Each resolution references an existing FollowUp, a nonempty list of distinct
Evidence IDs, and a nonempty note. A FollowUp can have one resolution; unknown
references, duplicates and additional fields are rejected. Resolution presence
is an authored declaration of completion, not an inference from a PDF path,
an inspection flag, a numerical pass or an Evidence category. Validation cannot
establish that the completion note is scientifically justified.

`explain` separates completed instructions from pending work, displays the note
and links to the completion evidence. JSON also retains the original instruction
under `resolved_follow_ups`. When all authored tasks on an Evidence item are
completed, its generic historical full-text acquisition suggestion is omitted;
an unchecked-comparison suggestion also moves to history when the completion
cites an inspected comparison against the same inspected source. Source identity
is determined by Evidence ID or matching artifact SHA-256, never by URL alone.
Only matching comparison targets move to `historical_steps`; other unchecked
targets, discrepancies and pending authored tasks remain visible. Agreement and
discrepancy both count as a recorded comparison, with different outcomes. The
display explains this distinction before showing the preserved record and links
to assessments citing the completion evidence. No historical source field,
provenance link or support Assessment changes. `--trace` and v0.1 restoration
therefore retain their original results. New reconstruction or calibration work
uses separate FollowUp IDs rather than being silently closed with the reading task.
